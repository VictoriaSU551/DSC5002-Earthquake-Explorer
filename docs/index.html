What the new comparisons show

70.15% of recorded earthquakes lie within 100 km of a mapped PB2002 boundary, versus 9.42% for equal-area globe points. This descriptive reference clarifies spatial concentration. Time trends are read alongside catalogue-comparability evidence; depth sections and stratified correlations reveal regional differences; cumulative case curves separate timing from totals.

All results describe a fixed catalogue. They do not estimate future hazard or establish causality.
